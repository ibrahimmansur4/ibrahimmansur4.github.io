About Me

I'm an electrical engineer (B.E., NUST) who fell in love with the space where software meets hardware. I started out building robots and ESP32-based IoT products, and today I develop software for medical devices — Qt-based HMIs and C++ device-control layers for dialysis systems, working within IEC 62304 / 60601 processes where design reviews, risk management, and code quality genuinely matter.

Along the way I've written firmware for smart water geysers, built localization systems for indoor delivery robots, trained CNNs to recognize human-object interactions, and put web servers on microcontrollers. I like systems that are intelligent, efficient, and dependable.
